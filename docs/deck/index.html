Profile

Computational designer & researcher in robotic fabrication and geometry.

I build the geometry, perception and motion systems that let machines fabricate with non-standard materials - irregular timber, salvaged stone, sheet metal, clay and concrete. The work spans graduate research, built pavilions, and an ecosystem of open-source tools for AEC.

Trained in architecture (B.Arch) and robotics & advanced construction (MRAC, IAAC Barcelona); currently researching computational architecture and robotics at Alfaisal University, Riyadh.
